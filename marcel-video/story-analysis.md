# Marcel AI — Story Analysis & Final Script

> Analysis performed manually (OpenAI credits exhausted). Grounded in `brand-kit/marcel-ai.md`.

## Draft critique vs. Brand Kit

**Strengths**
- Evidence-first structure: hook → product → outcome → trust → CTA.
- No hype, no guaranteed returns, no urgency — matches voice rules.
- "Every conclusion, backed by evidence you can see" maps directly to the **Transparency** pillar ("We show our work").

**Issues found**
1. "decisions on hype and headlines" — slightly negative framing of the market. Reframed to "noise and headlines" to stay objective (brand voice = confident, not combative).
2. The brand's core line *"Marcel doesn't pick winners. Marcel finds facts."* (from the **Trust** pillar) was missing — it's the strongest possible closer. Added to the CTA.
3. "coordinated AI analysts" needed a sharper verb — "deploys" matches the hero page copy ("deploys specialist AI agents").
4. Confidence score line ran long for TTS pacing; tightened to keep ~40s timing.

## Final narration (v1.0)

> Every day, retail investors make decisions on noise and headlines. Marcel changes that.
>
> Marcel deploys specialized AI analysts — each one examining financial health, fraud red flags, SEC filings, and insider activity.
>
> Then it synthesizes everything into one transparent rating: Buy, Hold, or Sell — with a confidence score and a plain-English explanation of exactly what's driving it.
>
> No black boxes. No hype. Every conclusion, backed by evidence you can see.
>
> Marcel doesn't pick winners. Marcel finds facts. Smart investing, explained.
