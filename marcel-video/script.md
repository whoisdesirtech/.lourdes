# Marcel AI — 40-Second Promo Script (Draft)

## Narration

[0-8s — Hook]
Every day, retail investors make decisions on hype and headlines. Marcel changes that.

[8-16s — Product]
Marcel coordinates specialized AI analysts — each one examining financials, fraud red flags, SEC filings, and insider activity.

[16-26s — Outcome]
Then it synthesizes everything into one transparent rating: Buy, Hold, or Sell — with a confidence score and a plain-English explanation of exactly what's driving it.

[26-34s — Trust]
No black boxes. No hype. Every conclusion, backed by evidence you can see.

[34-40s — CTA]
Marcel. Facts over fear. Smart investing, explained.
