## Scope Definition — Restaurant Website

**Prospect:** Restaurant / Food Service | 12 employees | Budget: $8k-$12k | Timeline: 6 weeks to grand opening

---

### Primary Deliverable

**Website Design & Development** — a responsive, premium-quality website showcasing the restaurant's menu, story, and brand identity.

---

### What It Includes

- Design brief intake and aesthetic alignment with restaurant brand
- Responsive layout (mobile, tablet, desktop) — critical for diners searching on phones
- Up to 5 pages: Home, Menu, Our Story, Gallery, Contact/Reservations
- Premium typography and color implementation matching restaurant vibe
- Subtle micro-animations for menu items and hero sections
- Performance-optimized HTML/CSS/JS delivery
- One round of design revisions

---

### Timeline

**4-6 weeks from kickoff** to launch-ready site.

Assumption: Client provides menu content, photos, and brand assets promptly. If photo shoot or copywriting is needed, timeline extends.

---

### Out of Scope

- Backend development or CMS integration (menu updates will need developer)
- Online ordering or reservation system integration
- SEO optimization beyond basics
- Stock photography procurement
- Ongoing hosting, maintenance, or menu updates
- Custom photography or videography

---

### Optional Secondary Services

- **Brand Kit Generation** — if restaurant lacks defined brand guidelines (colors, fonts, tone), recommend building a brand kit first to anchor the website design
- **Instagram Carousel Content** — promote grand opening with a carousel post for social media

---

### Alternatives Note

Prospect described a "premium" site. Could scope as landing page only (faster, cheaper) or full multi-page site. Scoped as multi-page based on stated needs (menu, story, contact). If budget is tight, recommend cutting to 3 core pages.

---

### Assumptions

1. **Assumed:** Restaurant has existing logo and brand colors — if not, recommend brand kit as prerequisite
2. **Assumed:** Client will provide professional food photography — if not, stock photos will be used (lower quality)
3. **Assumed:** "Premium" means clean, modern design — not ultra-luxury or fine dining aesthetic
4. **Assumed:** 6-week timeline is firm due to grand opening — flagged tension: 4-6 week range fits, but only if kickoff happens immediately and content is delivered on time
5. **Assumed:** No online ordering needed at launch — if needed, this is out of scope and requires separate engagement
6. **Flagged gap:** No mention of existing domain name or hosting — need to confirm before proposal
