# Brand Kit: Haitian Brand — Grand Opening & FIFA World Cup Showcase

> **Collaboration:** WhoIsDésir Media Agency × Haitian Brand
> **Event Date:** July 18, 2026
> **Coverage:** 6:00 – 8:00 PM | **Event:** 4:00 – 9:00 PM
> **Location:** South Florida Area
> **Production:** Two-Photographer Team (Lead + Assistant/Second Camera for social media)

---

## 1. Project Overview

- **Partnership Value:** WhoIsDésir Media Agency specializes in minority-owned businesses, delivering visual storytelling and reusable marketing assets rather than standard event photos.
- **Signature Menu Item:** Custom food styling and photography of signature menu items, including a specialty **Griot Pizza**.
- **Event Experience:** High-energy FIFA World Cup viewing experience, grand opening festivities, venue interior/exterior, and community engagement.
- **Marketing Scope:** Commercial usage license covering Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, websites, Google Business Profiles, press, and print marketing.
- **Community Visibility:** Cross-promotion and strategic community tagging targeting South Florida (Miami-Dade and Broward) audiences.

## 2. Color Palette: Miami Vice Aesthetic

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Vibrant Pink | `#FF1493` | Headers, accents, CTAs |
| **Secondary** | Miami Blue | `#00BFFF` | Supporting graphics, highlights |
| **Tertiary** | Deep Navy | `#0A0A2E` | Backgrounds, dark sections |
| **Accent** | Neon Cyan | `#00FFFF` | Border glows, energetic accents |
| **Accent** | Electric Violet | `#9B30FF` | Gradient transitions |
| **Neutral** | Warm White | `#FFF5EE` | Text on dark backgrounds |
| **Neutral** | Light Gray | `#F0F0F0` | Card backgrounds |

### Gradient Signature
```css
--gradient-miami-vice: linear-gradient(135deg, #FF1493, #9B30FF, #00BFFF);
--gradient-sunset: linear-gradient(135deg, #FF6B6B, #FF1493, #9B30FF);
```

## 3. Typography

| Role | Font | Fallback | Style |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headings** | `Oswald` or `Bebas Neue` | `sans-serif` | Uppercase, bold, compressed |
| **Subheadings** | `Outfit` | `sans-serif` | Semi-bold, clean |
| **Body** | `Inter` | `sans-serif` | Light to regular weight |
| **Accent/Script** | `Great Vibes` or `Pacifico` | `cursive` | For signature menu items (e.g., Griot Pizza) |

## 4. Brand Voice & Tone

### Tone Descriptors
Celebratory, High-energy, Professional, Culturally Proud, Community-focused

### On-Brand Language Themes
- "Grand opening celebration"
- "FIFA World Cup viewing experience"
- "Haitian flavors meet Miami energy"
- "Visual storytelling for minority-owned businesses"
- "South Florida's newest destination"

### Off-Brand Language
- Overly corporate or sterile descriptions
- Generic stock-photo language
- Cultural appropriation or misrepresentation

---

## 5. Visual Style Direction

### Photography Style
- **Food Photography:** Styled close-ups of Griot Pizza and signature menu items — warm lighting, shallow depth of field, condensation details on beverages
- **Event Photography:** Candid crowd shots, authentic reactions during World Cup viewing, high-energy moments
- **Venue:** Interior/exterior establishing shots, branded signage, atmospheric night lighting
- **Team:** Behind-the-scenes of two-photographer crew at work

### Color Grading
- Warm, saturated tones with teal/orange complementary balance
- Neon highlights (pink/blue) for night scenes and B-roll
- Clean, bright whites for daytime food shots

### Motion & Video
- Slight slow-motion for celebratory moments
- Quick cuts synced to upbeat music for TikTok/Reels
- Stabilized gimbal walkthroughs of venue
